Abstrak

Latar belakang: Permohonan pendanaan Global Fund Siklus 8 (2027 sampai 2029) untuk Indonesia perlu memaksimalkan dampak program HIV dalam amplop dana yang tetap. Analisis ini menilai apakah realokasi sumber daya yang ada, tanpa menambah anggaran, dapat meningkatkan penemuan kasus HIV. Metode: Analisis biaya terintegrasi dari perspektif program atas program HIV berbasis komunitas bagi laki-laki yang berhubungan seks dengan laki-laki (LSL), transgender, dan penasun yang didukung Yayasan Spiritia di 23 provinsi, Januari sampai Desember 2025. Biaya total USD 10,69 juta dari laporan keuangan dialokasikan secara top-down dan berbasis aktivitas ke lima intervensi dan tiga populasi. Data program berasal dari PUDR, SIHA, dan SIMS. Kasus terdeteksi diestimasi sebagai jumlah yang dites dikalikan positivitas notifikasi pasangan sebagai proksi. Skenario realokasi disusun dengan anggaran tetap dan diuji dengan analisis sensitivitas. Hasil: Sumber daya manusia menyerap 95% biaya. Biaya per tes sekitar USD 8. Notifikasi pasangan merupakan intervensi paling efisien, sekitar USD 49 per kasus terdeteksi, dibanding USD 1.063 sampai 1.401 untuk penjangkauan, tes, rujukan dan retensi, serta dukungan psikososial. LSL memiliki biaya per kasus terendah (sekitar USD 38 sampai 50) dengan positivitas 21,6%. Dengan anggaran tetap USD 10,69 juta per tahun, realokasi ke LSL dan notifikasi pasangan disertai peningkatan produktivitas SDM menaikkan kasus terdeteksi dari sekitar 38.000 menjadi 48.500 per tahun (28%), atau sekitar 31.500 kasus tambahan selama 2027 sampai 2029, dan menurunkan biaya per kasus sekitar 22%. Kesimpulan: Realokasi strategis dapat meningkatkan penemuan kasus secara bermakna tanpa menambah anggaran, asalkan disertai peningkatan produktivitas, perlindungan layanan bagi transgender dan penasun, serta pembelian berbasis kinerja.

Abstract

Background: Indonesia's Global Fund Grant Cycle 8 funding request (2027 to 2029) must maximise the impact of HIV programmes within a fixed envelope. This analysis assesses whether reallocating existing resources, without increasing the budget, can increase HIV case detection. Methods: An integrated costed analysis from the programme perspective of community-based HIV programmes for men who have sex with men (MSM), transgender people and people who inject drugs, supported by Spiritia Foundation in 23 provinces, January to December 2025. Total cost of USD 10.69 million from financial reports was allocated top-down and by activity to five interventions and three populations. Programme data came from PUDR, SIHA and SIMS. Cases detected were estimated as the number tested multiplied by partner notification positivity as a proxy. Reallocation scenarios were built with a fixed budget and tested in sensitivity analyses. Results: Human resources accounted for 95% of cost. Cost per test was about USD 8. Partner notification was the most efficient intervention, at about USD 49 per case detected, compared with USD 1,063 to 1,401 for outreach, testing, linkage and retention, and psychosocial support. MSM had the lowest cost per case (about USD 38 to 50) with 21.6% positivity. With a fixed budget of USD 10.69 million per year, reallocation toward MSM and partner notification combined with higher staff productivity raised cases detected from about 38,000 to 48,500 per year (28%), or about 31,500 additional cases over 2027 to 2029, and reduced cost per case by about 22%. Conclusions: Strategic reallocation can substantially increase case detection without additional budget, provided it is combined with productivity gains, protected services for transgender people and people who inject drugs, and performance-based purchasing.

Pesan kunci. Dengan anggaran tetap sekitar USD 10,69 juta per tahun, mengalihkan sumber daya ke populasi dan intervensi dengan hasil tertinggi dapat menaikkan penemuan kasus HIV sekitar 28%, dari kira-kira 38.000 menjadi 48.500 kasus per tahun, sekaligus menurunkan biaya per kasus sekitar 22%. Notifikasi pasangan adalah intervensi paling efisien, dan program bagi LSL memberi hasil per dolar tertinggi. Karena 95% biaya adalah sumber daya manusia, peningkatan produktivitas petugas sama pentingnya dengan pengalihan anggaran.

1. Konteks dan tujuan

Permohonan pendanaan Global Fund Siklus 8 untuk periode 2027 sampai 2029 disusun dalam amplop dana yang tidak bertambah. Pertanyaan perencanaannya menjadi lebih tajam: dengan uang yang sama, bagaimana menemukan lebih banyak orang dengan HIV yang belum terdiagnosis dan menghubungkan mereka ke pengobatan?

Analisis ini bertujuan menghasilkan bukti tentang biaya dan efisiensi program HIV berbasis komunitas lintas populasi kunci dan intervensi, sebagai dasar prioritas dan alokasi sumber daya dalam permohonan pendanaan. Lima pertanyaan dijawab:

  1. Populasi mana yang memberi biaya per kasus terdeteksi terendah?
  2. Intervensi mana yang paling efektif biaya dalam menemukan kasus?
  3. Apa pendorong utama biaya program?
  4. Seberapa efisien program mengubah penjangkauan menjadi hasil, dari dijangkau, dites, positif, sampai memulai ARV?
  5. Bagaimana sumber daya sebaiknya dialokasikan ulang untuk memaksimalkan dampak dan efisiensi?

2. Data dan metode

Cakupan analisis adalah program HIV berbasis komunitas bagi LSL, transgender, dan penasun yang didukung Yayasan Spiritia di 23 provinsi. Intervensi yang dianalisis meliputi penjangkauan, tes HIV di komunitas, fasilitas, dan secara mandiri, notifikasi pasangan, rujukan dan retensi, serta dukungan psikososial. Periode analisis adalah Januari sampai Desember 2025: biaya memakai data setahun penuh, sedangkan kinerja program memakai data semester kedua. Analisis memakai perspektif program atau penyedia layanan dan seluruh nilai dinyatakan dalam dolar Amerika Serikat dengan kurs Bank Indonesia.

Empat sumber data dipakai: laporan kemajuan dan permintaan pencairan (PUDR) Global Fund 2025, laporan keuangan program, data tes HIV dari SIHA, dan data program dari SIMS. Data diverifikasi kelengkapannya, diperiksa konsistensinya antarsistem, dibersihkan dari duplikasi, dan ditriangulasi antara SIHA dan SIMS.

Penghitungan biaya memakai pendekatan campuran. Biaya total diambil secara top-down dari laporan keuangan, lalu dialokasikan ke aktivitas secara proporsional terhadap keluarannya, dan ke populasi secara proporsional terhadap layanan yang diterima. Biaya tidak langsung dialokasikan menurut porsi biaya langsung, dan biaya modal disebar menurut porsi aktivitas. Diskonto tidak diterapkan karena horizon waktunya satu tahun. Tiga rumus inti dipakai:

  • biaya per tes adalah biaya total dibagi jumlah orang yang dites;
  • kasus terdeteksi adalah jumlah yang dites dikalikan tingkat positivitas;
  • biaya per kasus terdeteksi adalah biaya total dibagi jumlah kasus terdeteksi.

Karena konfirmasi diagnosis per kanal tidak selalu tersedia, positivitas notifikasi pasangan dipakai sebagai proksi hasil penemuan kasus, yaitu 18,1% secara keseluruhan dan 21,6% pada LSL. Analisis sensitivitas mengubah positivitas (10% sampai 25%), volume tes (plus minus 20%), biaya satuan (plus minus 15%), produktivitas SDM, dan porsi alokasi antarpopulasi.

Key message. With a fixed budget of about USD 10.69 million a year, shifting resources towards the populations and interventions with the highest yield can raise HIV case detection by about 28%, from roughly 38,000 to 48,500 cases a year, while cutting the cost per case by about 22%. Partner notification is the most efficient intervention, and programmes for MSM give the highest return per dollar. Because 95% of costs are staff, raising staff productivity matters as much as moving the budget.

1. Context and aims

Indonesia's funding request to the Global Fund for Grant Cycle 8, covering 2027 to 2029, is prepared within an envelope that will not grow. The planning question becomes sharper: with the same money, how can more people living with undiagnosed HIV be found and linked to treatment?

This analysis aims to generate evidence on the cost and efficiency of community-based HIV programmes across key populations and interventions, as a basis for prioritisation and resource allocation in the funding request. Five questions are answered:

  1. Which population yields the lowest cost per case detected?
  2. Which interventions are most cost-effective in finding cases?
  3. What drives the cost of the programme?
  4. How efficiently does the programme turn outreach into results, from reached, to tested, to positive, to starting ART?
  5. How should resources be reallocated to maximise impact and efficiency?

2. Data and methods

The analysis covers community-based HIV programmes for MSM, transgender people and people who inject drugs supported by Spiritia Foundation in 23 provinces. The interventions analysed are outreach, HIV testing in the community, in facilities and by self-testing, partner notification, linkage and retention, and psychosocial support. The period of analysis is January to December 2025: costs use full-year data, while programme performance uses data from the second semester. The analysis takes a programme or provider perspective, and all values are in US dollars at Bank Indonesia exchange rates.

Four data sources were used: the 2025 Global Fund progress update and disbursement request (PUDR), the programme financial report, HIV testing data from SIHA, and programme data from SIMS. Data were checked for completeness and for consistency across systems, cleaned of duplicates, and triangulated between SIHA and SIMS.

Costing used a mixed approach. Total cost was taken top-down from the financial report, then allocated to activities in proportion to their outputs and to populations in proportion to the services they received. Indirect costs were allocated by share of direct costs, and capital costs were spread by share of activity. No discounting was applied because the time horizon is one year. Three core formulas were used:

  • cost per test is total cost divided by the number of people tested;
  • cases detected are the number tested multiplied by the positivity rate;
  • cost per case detected is total cost divided by the number of cases detected.

Because confirmed diagnoses by channel are not always available, partner notification positivity was used as a proxy for case finding yield: 18.1% overall and 21.6% among MSM. Sensitivity analyses varied positivity (10% to 25%), testing volume (plus or minus 20%), unit costs (plus or minus 15%), staff productivity, and the share of resources allocated across populations.

Quarterly costs rose from USD 2.32 million (January to March) to 2.54, 2.73 and 3.10 million, in line with programme expansion and partner notification activity.

Within staff costs, outreach workers and peer educators account for 46.2% of total cost, followed by programme managers and supervisors (18.7%), counsellors and case managers (14.3%), data and monitoring and evaluation staff (8.6%), support functions (7.2%), and other staff (5.0%). The main cost drivers are the number of outreach workers and the geographic coverage across 23 provinces. Efficiency is therefore more likely to come from raising staff productivity than from cutting staff numbers. The sensitivity analysis estimates that a 15% to 30% increase in staff productivity could lower the cost per case detected by 22% to 40%.

Partner notification is about 21 times more cost-effective than facility and community testing, and about 27 times more cost-effective than psychosocial support. In the incremental analysis, every other intervention is dominated by partner notification, that is, more costly and finding fewer cases. Even so, outreach still contributes the most cases after partner notification and is the entry point for people who have never been reached. Linkage, retention and psychosocial support are judged by the same yardstick, even though their main purpose is to keep people on treatment, so their cost per case should be read with care.

8. Rekomendasi

  • Prioritaskan investasi bagi LSL. Skala terbesar dan hasil tertinggi di antara populasi kunci, dengan biaya per kasus terendah. Pada semester kedua 2025, 237.195 LSL dijangkau dan 233.232 dites, dengan biaya per tes sekitar USD 8.
  • Perluas notifikasi pasangan. Pada semester kedua 2025, 13.465 pasangan dijangkau, 12.832 dites, positivitas 18,1% (21,6% pada LSL), dan 92,7% yang terdiagnosis memulai ARV. Lembagakan notifikasi pasangan di semua titik layanan tes.
  • Beralih ke strategi tes berhasil tinggi. Fokus pada jaringan dan orang berisiko tinggi yang tes berulang, optimalkan campuran tes di fasilitas, komunitas, dan tes mandiri, dan perbaiki konversi dari penjangkauan ke tes.
  • Naikkan produktivitas SDM. Tambah kontak per petugas dan produktivitas tes, manfaatkan platform digital, dan gunakan data untuk penajaman sasaran serta manajemen kinerja.
  • Pertahankan dukungan terarah bagi transgender dan penasun. Jaga kesinambungan layanan esensial, pakai penajaman sasaran mikro di titik panas dan jaringan, dan perkuat rujukan ke layanan serta retensi.

Untuk Siklus 8, analisis ini mengusulkan tiga tingkat prioritas: perluasan intervensi berdampak tinggi (penjangkauan dan tes yang berfokus pada LSL, notifikasi pasangan, dan tes yang tertarget), optimalisasi efisiensi (penjangkauan digital, produktivitas penjangkauan, dan sasaran titik panas), serta pengendalian epidemi yang berkelanjutan (layanan transgender dan penasun, rujukan ke ARV, retensi, dan kepatuhan). Arah perubahannya adalah dari penjangkauan umum ke populasi berhasil tinggi, dari intervensi berhasil rendah ke notifikasi pasangan dan tes tertarget, dari alokasi seragam ke sasaran berbasis data, dan dari perencanaan berbasis masukan ke pembelian berbasis kinerja.

9. Catatan metodologis dan keterbatasan

Kasus terdeteksi adalah estimasi dari positivitas notifikasi pasangan, bukan diagnosis terkonfirmasi, sehingga angka absolut kasus pada Tabel 2 dan Tabel 4 perlu dibaca sebagai indikasi besaran, bukan hitungan. Kedua kerangka skenario memakai titik awal yang berbeda, yaitu sekitar 38.000 kasus dengan biaya per kasus USD 282 dan 32.539 kasus dengan biaya per kasus USD 328, sehingga persentase kenaikannya (28% dan 21,5%) tidak dapat dijumlahkan atau dibandingkan langsung. Metode menyatakan hubungan linear antara volume dan biaya, tetapi hasil skenario lebih sesuai dengan hasil yang makin menurun, sebagaimana ditunjukkan kalkulator pada bagian 7. Biaya mencerminkan perspektif program berbasis komunitas dan tidak mencakup seluruh biaya sistem kesehatan. Hasil berlaku untuk periode Januari sampai Desember 2025 dan dapat dipengaruhi pelaporan yang kurang dan variasi mutu data rutin.

8. Recommendations

  • Prioritise investment in MSM. The largest scale and highest yield among key populations, with the lowest cost per case. In the second semester of 2025, 237,195 MSM were reached and 233,232 tested, at a cost per test of about USD 8.
  • Scale up partner notification. In the second semester of 2025, 13,465 partners were reached and 12,832 tested, with 18.1% positivity (21.6% among MSM), and 92.7% of those diagnosed started ART. Institutionalise partner notification at every testing point.
  • Shift to high-yield testing strategies. Focus on networks and on high-risk repeat testers, optimise the mix of facility, community and self-testing, and improve conversion from outreach to testing.
  • Raise staff productivity. Increase contacts per worker and testing productivity, use digital platforms, and use data for targeting and performance management.
  • Maintain targeted support for transgender people and people who inject drugs. Keep essential services going, use micro-targeting in hotspots and networks, and strengthen linkage to care and retention.

For Grant Cycle 8, the analysis proposes three priority tiers: scaling up high-impact interventions (MSM-focused outreach and testing, partner notification and targeted testing), optimising efficiency (digital outreach, outreach productivity and hotspot targeting), and sustained epidemic control (services for transgender people and people who inject drugs, linkage to ART, retention and adherence). The direction of change is from general outreach to high-yield populations, from low-yield interventions to partner notification and targeted testing, from uniform allocation to data-driven targeting, and from input-based planning to performance-based purchasing.

9. Methodological notes and limitations

Cases detected are estimates based on partner notification positivity, not confirmed diagnoses, so the absolute case numbers in Table 2 and Table 4 should be read as an indication of magnitude rather than a count. The two scenario frameworks start from different baselines, about 38,000 cases at USD 282 per case and 32,539 cases at USD 328 per case, so their percentage gains (28% and 21.5%) cannot be added or compared directly. The methods state a linear relationship between volume and cost, but the scenario results fit diminishing returns better, as the calculator in section 7 shows. Costs reflect the perspective of community-based programmes and do not include all health system costs. Results apply to January to December 2025 and may be affected by under-reporting and variable quality of routine data.
